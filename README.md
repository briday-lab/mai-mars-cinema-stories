# Mai Mars Cinema Stories

"Create a professional, cinematic portfolio website for a Montreal-based film production company called 'Les Productions du Mai Mars'.

Who is it for: Film festival programmers, international co-producers, and financiers looking for high-quality, independent auteur cinema with African and diasporic roots.

Design vibe: The look should be premium, moody, and cinematic. Use a dark mode color palette (blacks, deep slate greys, and muted gold or silver accents) with plenty of negative space. The typography should be an elegant, modern serif font. The layout should be visually driven, letting the film imagery stand out.

Must-have features:

A striking homepage with a full-width video background or high-impact image slider showcasing our films.

A 'Projects' or 'Slate' page featuring our current films (Muntu, Inkoni, and Scène de Rêve) with space for embedded Vimeo trailers and loglines.

An 'About Us' section highlighting our focus on international co-productions between Canada, the Global South, and Africa, including a brief bio for the producers.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on Netlify at https://maimars.com. Every push to `main` deploys to production automatically; pull requests get a preview deploy.

## Development

You need [Bun](https://bun.sh) (or Node.js and npm).

```sh
bun install
bun run dev
```

Photos live in `public/images/` and are served directly by the site.
